4 Easy Steps

  1. Browse our menu and choose your coffee and snacks.
  2. Message us on Facebook - Kape Tankulan page or call 0912-345-6789.
  3. Confirm your order, delivery or pick-up, and payment method (GCash / COD).
  4. Enjoy! Wait for your order in 15-20 minutes. Pick-up at Poblacion, Manolo Fortich.

Opening Hours

We are open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM. Closed on Sundays for family time.

Find Us

Located at Poblacion, Manolo Fortich, Bukidnon, near the Municipal Plaza.
